# 1. Geography

Help users learn where a country is, what its land and climate are like, and how these affect where people live and work.

## What users can learn

- **Location:** neighbours, seas, islands and distances.
- **Land:** mountains, plains, valleys and coastlines.
- **Water:** rivers, lakes and underground water.
- **Climate:** seasons, usual weather patterns and long-term changes.
- **Resources and land use:** minerals, farmland, forests, towns and protected areas.
- **Environment:** wildlife, conservation and published studies of environmental risks.

Government areas, natural regions and wildlife areas may have different boundaries. Explain what each map shows.

## How users can explore

| User wants to             | The app can show                                         |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Find a country            | A map of the country and its surroundings                |
| Explore a river or region | A selected shape with details and sources                |
| Compare places            | Matching maps, charts or tables                          |
| See changes               | A timeline or maps from different dates, where available |

Explain unfamiliar words when they appear. For example, a catchment is the area that drains water into a river.

## Example with AI

**Question:** "Show the main rivers and explain where people live."

AI checks whether the app has reliable river maps and population information for the selected country. It shows the supported layers, focuses on a useful area and explains the sources.

Clicking a river can lead to "Where does it flow?" or "Which places are beside it?" If only country boundaries are available, AI explains that limit. It must not draw guessed rivers or claim that a nearby river supplies a town.

## Information and app changes needed

Use checked information from mapping, environment, geology and weather agencies, plus research that explains its methods. Save the source, date, map accuracy, original IDs and reuse terms.

Land, climate and environmental-risk layers need new data and map controls. Keep a long-term climate average separate from a forecast, and a risk study separate from a current warning.

Show who supplied each boundary and which version it is. Different sources may draw coastlines or disputed areas differently.

## What already works

The app has country boundaries, dated regional maps, South African municipal and ward boundaries, map controls and online street detail. Its [satellite view](../satellite-view.md) shows real 2016 images at about 10 m ground detail without a key. A separate map key can enable sharper imagery. These are captured images, not live views or proof of what a place is called. Most named land, climate, river and environmental learning layers still need to be added.

## How to check it

Users should be able to find a place, understand the type of area shown and explain one fact using a source. Check that selected map items open the correct details, credits are visible and missing layers are explained.
